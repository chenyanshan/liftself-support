# Public documentation changelog

This records publisher-confirmed changes. It does not declare an App Store or Google Play release.

## 2026-11-23

- Published this official support/documentation repository, product facts, Strong/Hevy CSV migration guide and data/backup boundaries.
- Current iOS submission is 1.0.0 (18), awaiting review with manual public release retained. Public testing remains separate from store availability.

## 2026-11-22

- Current product policy includes unlimited free workout templates. Free custom exercises remain limited to seven and free charts to 30 days.
- Migration workflow can save a selected imported workout as an editable template, including a record already imported from the selected file. Availability depends on the installed beta version; the older Android download may differ from the latest iOS candidate.

Use the [official product page](https://hihihiai.com/solift) for current availability and the [support page](https://hihihiai.com/solift/support) for behavior. Beta changes are not evidence that every device/store/cloud scenario has been verified.
