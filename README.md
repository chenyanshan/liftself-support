# LiftSelf — Offline Gym & Strength Training Log

**Lift for yourself.**

LiftSelf is an ad-free, local-first strength training log for iOS and Android by **HIHIHIAI Studio**. It records workouts offline without a LiftSelf account and imports supported Strong and Hevy workout-history CSVs. Core logging is free; optional Pro uses a one-time purchase instead of a subscription.

This is the publisher-maintained documentation and feedback repository for the LiftSelf app at **[hihihiai.com/liftself](https://hihihiai.com/liftself)**.

LiftSelf for Android is available from the [official download page](https://hihihiai.com/liftself#download) as a signed ARM64 APK: version 1.0.0 (20), Android 7.0 or later. This direct download does not offer Pro purchases and is not a Google Play release.

The launch period starts with public App Store availability, not each person’s installation. It is not a 30-day free trial. Store purchases belong to their original store account; Apple and Google purchases are separate. A journal backup does not transfer Pro.

## Moving from Strong or Hevy

Keep your original export and existing app until you have checked the result. Importing CSV transfers supported completed workout records, not the original account, saved routine library or every attachment. After importing, you can choose a workout from that file, edit it and save it as a LiftSelf template. Follow the [step-by-step migration guide](guides/strong-hevy-import.md).

LiftSelf focuses on private offline logging. This does not mean Strong or Hevy lacks offline support or lifetime offers: check each product’s current official information. There is no claim of complete feature parity.

## Feedback

[Open an issue](https://github.com/chenyanshan/liftself-support/issues/new/choose) for a reproducible bug or feature request. Include app version/build, platform, device, steps, expected behavior and actual result. **Issues and attachments are public. Do not upload full workout exports, backups, receipts, Apple/Google account details or health data.** Use invented sample values and crop/redact screenshots.

For private support, contact **support@hihihiai.com**. Start with a description and minimal redacted example; do not send a full journal. Store refunds are handled by Apple or Google. See the [official support page](https://hihihiai.com/liftself/support#restore).
